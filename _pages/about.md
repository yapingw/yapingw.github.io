Welcome to my webpage!

I am an Assistant Professor in Economics at the [University of Nottingham Ningbo China](https://www.nottingham.edu.cn/en/humanities-and-social-sciences/schools-and-department/economics/home.aspx).

I received my Ph.D in Economics from  [Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF)](https://www.upf.edu/en/web/econ) and [Barcelona School of Economics (BSE)](https://bse.eu/) in 2026, under the supervision of [Prof. Christian Brownlees](https://ctbrownlees.github.io/).

My primary research field is econometric theory, focusing on high-dimensional modeling, factor models, quantile regression, and forecasting techniques, with additional interests in machine learning and empirical finance.

My CV is [here](https://yapingw.github.io/assets/pdf/cv.pdf).
